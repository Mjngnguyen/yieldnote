AI tools save the most time on repeatable writing, research and admin. Pick tools by the task, not the hype.

- Writing: drafts, outlines and edits for posts and emails.
- Research: summarising long documents and comparing sources.
- Design: social images, thumbnails and mockups.
- Automation: connecting forms, email and spreadsheets.

> **Tip:** Always fact-check AI output before publishing. Search engines and readers both punish errors.
