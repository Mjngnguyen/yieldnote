The 50/30/20 budget splits take-home pay into needs, wants and savings. It is popular because it takes five minutes to set up.

- 50% needs: housing, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum debt payments.
- 30% wants: dining out, subscriptions, travel, hobbies.
- 20% savings and extra debt payments.

## When the numbers don’t fit

In high-rent cities needs can exceed 60%. Shrink wants first, keep at least 10% for savings, and revisit when income rises.

> **Tip:** Move savings on payday automatically. What you never see, you rarely spend.
