Hosting affects page speed, which affects search rankings and ad revenue. Cheap shared plans are fine at the start; move up when traffic grows.

- Check the renewal price, not just the first-year discount.
- Look for daily backups and free SSL.
- Pick a data centre close to your audience.
- Static sites like this one can be hosted free on Netlify, Vercel or GitHub Pages.
