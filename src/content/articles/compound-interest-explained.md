Compounding means your returns earn returns. The effect is small in early years and large in later ones, which is why starting early matters so much.

Run the calculator below with your own numbers. Then try starting five years later and compare the ending balance.

## The rule of 72

Divide 72 by your annual return to estimate how many years it takes money to double. At 8%, that is about nine years.
