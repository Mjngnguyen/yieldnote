Credit models differ by country, but the widely used FICO model publishes its weightings, and they are a good mental map.

- Payment history: about 35%. One missed payment hurts more than anything else.
- Amounts owed: about 30%. Keep card balances low relative to limits.
- Length of history: about 15%. Keep old accounts open.
- New credit: about 10%. Space out applications.
- Credit mix: about 10%. Minor; never borrow just to improve it.

> **Tip:** Set every card to autopay at least the minimum. It removes the biggest risk to your score.

## How fast it recovers

Paying balances down shows up as soon as the lender reports, often within a month. Late payments stay on your report for years but matter less as they age.
