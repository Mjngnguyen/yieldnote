If someone else holds your keys, you hold an IOU. That is fine for small trading amounts and risky for savings.

- Exchange: convenient, exposed to hacks and insolvency.
- Hot wallet (phone or browser): you hold keys, but the device is online.
- Cold wallet (hardware): keys stay offline; best for long-term holdings.

## Seed phrase rules

- Write it on paper or metal. Never photograph or type it.
- No support agent will ever ask for it.
- Store a copy in a second secure location.

Crypto is highly volatile and lightly regulated in many places. Only invest what you can afford to lose.
