description: "You own the list. Platforms can change the rules overnight."

Social reach depends on an algorithm you don’t control. An email list reaches subscribers directly, and it can be sold to sponsors, used to launch products and moved between providers.

- Offer one specific lead magnet, such as a checklist or template.
- Send on a fixed schedule so readers expect you.
- Mix 80% useful content with 20% offers.
