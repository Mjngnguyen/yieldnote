title: "How big should your emergency fund be?"
description: "Three to six months of essential expenses covers most people."

An emergency fund keeps a job loss or a car repair from turning into credit card debt.

- 3 months if you have stable income and a second earner.
- 6 months if you are the only earner or work in a volatile field.
- 9–12 months if you are self-employed.

Base the target on essential expenses only, not your full spending.
