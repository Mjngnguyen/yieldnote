Search job boards in your target market and count how often a skill appears in listings. Demand is the best predictor of pay.

- Data analysis and SQL
- Cloud and DevOps
- Product and UX design
- Performance marketing
- Sales and account management

> **Tip:** Build two portfolio projects while you learn. Employers hire from proof, not certificates alone.
