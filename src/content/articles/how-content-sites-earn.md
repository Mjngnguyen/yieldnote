A content site earns by turning attention into one of a few payouts. The mix matters more than raw traffic.

- Display ads: paid per 1,000 pageviews (RPM). Easy to add, depends heavily on topic and audience country.
- Affiliate: a commission per sale or lead. Best on comparison and how-to pages.
- Email: lets you earn from the same reader many times.
- Digital products: highest margin once built.
- Sponsorships: flat fees once you have an engaged audience.
