An index fund buys every company in a market index, such as the S&P 500 or a total world index. You get the market’s return minus a small fee.

## A simple starting setup

- Open a tax-advantaged account if your country offers one.
- Pick one global or total-market index fund with a low expense ratio.
- Add a bond fund if you need lower volatility.
- Automate a monthly contribution and leave it alone.

> **Tip:** Fees compound too. A 1% annual fee can consume a large share of a portfolio’s growth over 30 years.

Not financial advice. Markets fall as well as rise; invest money you will not need for at least five years.
