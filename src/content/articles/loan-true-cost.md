Lenders lead with the monthly payment because it is the number that feels affordable. The number to compare is total cost.

## Three numbers that matter

- APR includes interest plus most fees, so it compares offers fairly.
- Term is how long you pay. Longer terms lower the payment and raise total interest.
- Total repaid is payment × number of months. Compare this across offers.

> **Tip:** Check for prepayment penalties. Being able to pay early for free is worth a slightly higher rate.
