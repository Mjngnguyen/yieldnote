description: "Most savings come from shopping around and adjusting your deductible."

Insurers price the same driver very differently. The single most effective habit is comparing quotes every renewal.

- Compare at least three quotes for identical coverage.
- Raise your deductible to an amount you could pay tomorrow.
- Bundle home or renters cover with the same insurer.
- Ask about low-mileage and telematics discounts.
- Drop collision cover on an older car worth little.
- Pay annually if a monthly plan carries a fee.
- Keep your credit healthy where insurers are allowed to use it.

> **Tip:** Never cut liability limits to save money. That is the cover that protects your savings in a lawsuit.
