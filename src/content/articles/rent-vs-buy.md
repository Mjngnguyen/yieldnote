Rent is money you don’t get back. So are mortgage interest, property tax, maintenance and the opportunity cost of your down payment.

## The 5% rule of thumb

Multiply the home price by 5% and divide by 12. If you can rent a similar home for less than that each month, renting is often the better deal. The 5% roughly covers tax, maintenance and the cost of capital.

> **Tip:** Buying usually pays only if you stay long enough, often five years or more, to recover transaction costs.
