---
title: "Gross vs net rental yield"
description: "Gross yield flatters a property. Net yield tells you if it works."
topic: real-estate
pubDate: 2026-10-04
offers: [realEstate]
---

Gross yield is annual rent divided by purchase price. Net yield subtracts every running cost first.

- Vacancy: budget at least a few weeks a year.
- Maintenance: often 1% of property value per year.
- Management fees, insurance, property tax and service charges.

> **Tip:** Run the numbers at a higher interest rate than today’s to check the property still covers its costs.
