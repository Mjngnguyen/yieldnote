## Low skill, fast start

- Online surveys and microtasks: very low pay, useful only for spare minutes.
- Reselling second-hand items.
- Local services: pet care, cleaning, delivery.

## Medium skill

- Freelance writing, editing or translation.
- Social media management for small businesses.
- Virtual assistance.

## High skill, best long-term

- Web development, design or data analysis.
- Content sites and newsletters that earn from ads, affiliates and products.
- Online courses and templates.

Avoid anything that asks you to pay upfront to earn, recruit others to recover your fee, or promises guaranteed returns.
