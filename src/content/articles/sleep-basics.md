- Keep the same wake-up time every day, weekends included.
- Get daylight within an hour of waking.
- Stop caffeine at least 8 hours before bed.
- Keep the bedroom cool, dark and quiet.
- Most adults need 7–9 hours.

Persistent insomnia or loud snoring with daytime sleepiness are reasons to see a doctor.
