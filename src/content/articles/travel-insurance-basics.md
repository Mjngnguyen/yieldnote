A hospital stay abroad can cost more than the trip itself. Medical and evacuation cover are the essentials.

- Usually covered: emergency medical care, evacuation, trip cancellation for listed reasons, lost baggage.
- Often excluded: pre-existing conditions (unless declared), extreme sports, travel against government advice.

> **Tip:** Buy cover when you book, so cancellation protection starts immediately.
