title: "Travel rewards cards: when they’re worth it"
description: "Only if you pay in full every month. Interest wipes out any reward."
topic: travel
pubDate: 2026-10-04
offers: [travelCard]
---

Rewards cards return value as points or miles. They work for people who already pay their balance in full and travel at least once or twice a year.

- Compare the annual fee with the value you will realistically redeem.
- Look for no foreign transaction fees.
- Transferable points are usually worth more than fixed-value points.
